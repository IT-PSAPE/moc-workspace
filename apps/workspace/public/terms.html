Terms of use

These terms cover MOC Workspace and its public request portal, operated for the PSAPE Ministry of Culture team.

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Purpose and access

Use the service for legitimate team work and requests. Workspace access is limited to approved members and their assigned permissions. Google authentication alone does not grant workspace access. The team may change or revoke access.

Your responsibilities

  • Keep your Google account, active sessions and private request tracking codes secure.
  • Provide accurate work information and submit only material you are permitted to share.
  • Do not impersonate others, attempt unauthorized access, interfere with the service or submit abusive or unlawful material.
  • Avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for the request or task.

Work records and external services

Submitted requests and comments are made available to authorized team members for coordination and reporting. Public tracking information is available to anyone holding the private tracking code. Google, Supabase, Vercel and any enabled Telegram integrations have their own terms and policies for the services they provide.

Testing and availability

The service is currently being tested. Features, access and availability may change, and interruptions or errors can occur. Keep a separate copy of information you cannot replace and report problems to the team.

Privacy and contact

See the privacy notice for information handling. For questions about access, requests or these terms, contact psape.dev@gmail.com.
