MOC Workspace

MOC Workspace helps the PSAPE Ministry of Culture team review requests, assign work, update progress and keep project comments together.

For your team

Invited, approved team members sign in with Google. Access depends on the member’s department and leadership permissions.

For requesters

The public request portal lets you submit a request without a Google account. It provides a private tracking code to check your request and edit it while it awaits review.

Google sign-in

Google sign-in identifies your account. MOC Workspace requests basic identity information: your account identifier, email address, name and profile information. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Google Calendar or contacts.
