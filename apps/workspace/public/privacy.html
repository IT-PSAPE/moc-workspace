Privacy notice

This notice explains how the PSAPE Ministry of Culture team uses information in MOC Workspace and its public request portal.

Last updated: 4 November 2026.

Information we process

  • Google identity: Google and Supabase Auth process your account identifier, email address, name and profile information to complete sign-in. The app matches your verified email to an approved member record.
  • Team records: member names, email addresses, departments, groups and access permissions.
  • Work records: requests, instructions, tasks, assignments, statuses, dates, comments, activity records, notifications and generated reports.
  • Sessions and service operation: authentication cookies and technical information processed by our hosting and authentication providers to run and protect the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate members, apply access permissions, coordinate work, track requests, produce team reports and operate the service. Google sign-in requests basic identity scopes; the app does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Google Calendar or contacts.

Access and service providers

Approved team members can access work information according to their role and department. Cell 0 leaders manage membership and request review. Anyone holding a request’s private tracking code can view the public tracking information and edit the request while it awaits review, so keep that code confidential.

Google provides sign-in. Supabase provides authentication, database, private report storage and live updates. Vercel hosts the app and API. When the team enables Telegram notifications, short work summaries are sent to its configured Telegram chats. These providers process information as part of delivering their services.

Storage and security

The configured Supabase project is in Frankfurt, Germany. Providers may process other service and account information in locations covered by their own policies. The app uses API-side access checks, HttpOnly session cookies, encrypted database connections and private report storage. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and requests

Work records remain in the service until they are removed or managed by authorized operators. Some historical work and comments can remain after a member loses access. Contact the team to request access, correction or deletion of information, or to ask about retention. Requests will be reviewed in light of operational needs and applicable requirements.

Contact and changes

Contact psape.dev@gmail.com about privacy or access. This notice may be updated as the service changes; the current version will be available on this page.
